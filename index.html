About

The short version of five years of production code.

I'm a frontend developer who ended up specializing in the hardest part of mobile development: what happens when the connection drops. Most of my recent work has been building field and workforce apps that have to keep functioning — capturing data, tracking location, syncing later — in places where signal isn't guaranteed.

Before that, I spent time building the web side of enterprise systems: LMS, HRMS, and eCommerce platforms in React.js, focused on reusable components and performance. I care about code that's still easy to read six months later, not just code that ships on time.
